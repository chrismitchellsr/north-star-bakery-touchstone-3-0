Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to give the neighborhood a welcoming place for dependable, handmade baked goods. Our goal is to make everyday breakfasts, family celebrations, and community gatherings feel a little more special.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We focus on quality ingredients, thoughtful preparation, and seasonal choices. Whenever practical, we choose ingredients and suppliers that support freshness, consistency, and the local community.

Meet the Team

Our bakers prepare breads and pastries in small batches, while our front-of-house team helps customers choose products, plan event orders, and arrange convenient pickups.
